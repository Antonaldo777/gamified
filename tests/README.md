# Browser checks

1. Start the local website and open it in a browser. Check that all four stages
   are described in plain farm language.
2. In Stage 1, save a field note, save and undo a check, add and mark a planned
   visit, and work out a water amount. Refresh and confirm notes and visits are
   still there.
3. Try to finish Stage 1 without marking the farm tasks done. Confirm the reward
   stays locked. Mark every task, answer at least two questions correctly, and
   confirm the points and badge are awarded only once.
4. In Stage 2, try the sample reading views and explore related farm practices.
   Confirm the controls do not show technical terms.
5. In Stage 3, arrange and find sample readings. Confirm the results use plain
   language and clearly identify sample values.
6. In Stage 4, choose a water amount and fields. Confirm the sample plan stays
   within the amount and clearly says that it is not farming advice.
7. Refresh and confirm task progress, points, and badges remain saved. Save a
   copy of the journey, then reset and confirm field notes, visits, and rewards
   are cleared.
8. Check the website at desktop and narrow mobile widths.
