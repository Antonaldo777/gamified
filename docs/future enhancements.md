Future improvements for Fieldcraft: 

1)Keep the app’s wording simple and farmer-friendly. Let farmers add their own fields, crops, and farming activities, then record completed work with dates and optional notes or photos. 

2)Add a seasonal task calendar with reminders for activities such as checking soil, preparing compost, rotating crops, and monitoring water use. 

3)Allow farmers to choose their preferred language and measurement units. Add an optional way to save or back up progress so it can be restored on another device, while keeping personal information private.

4)Provide clear progress summaries that show completed tasks, earned points, and badges. Add more sustainable farming tips that are checked by trusted local farming experts and suited to the farmer’s region.

5)Clearly label examples as examples, and never present the app’s sample plans as a substitute for local agricultural advice.

6)The app should remain easy to use on phones, work reliably with a slow or unavailable internet connection, and explain what information is saved and where.
