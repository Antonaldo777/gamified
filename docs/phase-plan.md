# Farm task stages

The stages are sequential: the next stage unlocks after the farmer confirms all
its tasks and passes the short quiz. A stage awards its points and badge once.
The farmer confirms tasks themselves; Fieldcraft cannot verify work on the
farm.

## Stage 1 — Keep clear field notes

- Record a field observation.
- Check a field and note what needs attention.
- Plan which field to visit first.
- Work out an amount of water for a field task when known.

## Stage 2 — Notice crop and soil connections

- Compare example soil-moisture readings.
- Find a reading and decide whether to follow up.
- Explore general examples of connected soil-care practices.

## Stage 3 — Find patterns in field readings

- Arrange example readings from low to high.
- Find a reading and see which field it belongs to.
- Compare two ways to organize and find readings.

## Stage 4 — Plan careful water use

- Try a sample watering plan with selected fields and an example water amount.
- Review the example and consider what local field checks are needed before
  making a real watering decision.
- Show clearly that the sample plan does not know actual crop or field needs.

## Rewards and saved progress

Each stage has a self-confirmed farm-task checklist and a three-question quiz.
Passing requires at least two correct answers. Finishing the tasks and quiz
unlocks the stage badge and one-time points. Farm notes, visit plans, and
progress are stored only in the current browser on this device. Sample readings
and water amounts are learning examples, not farming advice.
